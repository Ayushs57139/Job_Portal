✅ FEATURE IMPLEMENTED: Company Labels System

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 WHAT IT DOES:

   Allows admins to assign special badges to companies:
   
   ⭐ Premium Company
   ⭐ Starred Company
   🎗️ Featured Company
   💼 Actively Hiring
   🚨 Urgent Company
   ✓ Verified Employer
   🏆 Top Rated

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🚀 HOW TO USE:

   1. Go to Users → Companies or Consultancies tab
   2. Find the company you want to label
   3. Click the tag icon (🏷️) in Actions column
   4. Select labels by clicking on them
   5. Click "Save Labels"
   6. Done! Labels appear immediately
